# Objectives
# The objectives of purity monitoring are to ensure that a substance or product meets required quality, safety, and efficacy standards. This practice is critical across various industries to protect health, maintain product consistency, and comply with regulatory guidelines. 
## •Quality Control and Assurance: To determine the precise composition of a substance and detect the presence or identity of any unintended impurities, contaminants, or adulterations. This ensures the final product is reliable and performs as expected. 
## •Accurate Research and Analysis: In scientific research and chemical analysis, using high-purity substances ensures accurate, reliable, and reproducible results that can be used as analytical standards for comparison with unknown samples. 
## •Optimized Manufacturing and Processing: For specific technical domains like semiconductor manufacturing or industrial operations, monitoring material purity ensures proper functioning of devices and optimal process conditions, preventing equipment issues or product failure.
